# AWS & DevOps Internship | Day 8 – Amazon EC2 Fundamentals

Continuing my AWS & DevOps Internship learning journey.

Today's session focused on Amazon EC2 and the concepts involved in launching and configuring a cloud-based virtual server.

**Key topics covered:**
- What an EC2 instance is and its role in AWS Compute
- The eight steps involved in launching an EC2 instance
- Amazon Machine Images (AMIs) and their categories
- Instance types, sizes, and instance families
- EC2 pricing options, including On-Demand, Reserved, Dedicated, and Spot
- Key pairs for secure SSH access
- Security Groups as stateful firewalls
- EC2 instance states: running, stopped, and terminated

**Key learning:** I gained a clearer understanding of the decisions involved before launching an EC2 instance—from selecting an operating system and instance size to configuring access and choosing a pricing option. I also learned why key pairs and security group rules are important for secure access.

Looking forward to applying these concepts in the upcoming practical session.
