# AWS & DevOps Internship | Day 1 – Understanding Cloud Computing & AWS Pricing

Started my AWS & DevOps Internship journey with a foundation session on cloud computing and the basics of Amazon Web Services (AWS).

In our first class, we explored:

**Cloud Computing**  
Understanding how computing resources such as servers, storage, databases, and networking are delivered over the internet.

**Cloud Service Models**
- IaaS – Infrastructure as a Service
- PaaS – Platform as a Service
- SaaS – Software as a Service

**AWS Service Categories**  
An introduction to Compute, Storage, Databases, Networking, Management, and Security services.

**AWS Pricing**  
Learned about the pay-as-you-go model, compute and storage costs, data transfer charges, and purchasing options such as On-Demand and Reserved Instances.

**Key takeaway:** Cloud computing is not just about accessing servers over the internet. Understanding service models, resource management, and pricing is equally important when choosing the right cloud solution.

Looking forward to building on these fundamentals through the practical tasks ahead.
